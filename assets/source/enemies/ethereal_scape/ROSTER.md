# Ethereal Scape enemy roster (base chunk set only)

Drafted 2026-09-26, alongside the world's 30-piece chunk kit (see
`docs/biomes/ETHEREAL_SCAPE.md`). Same universal rules as every other biome's
roster (`docs/ENEMY_FRAMEWORK.md`):
- Basic/miniboss/legendary tri budgets: basic 10k-12.5k, miniboss <=35k, boss <=75k. Every mesh <10k tris.
- Rig: humanoid enemies get R15 core bones; minibosses and bosses get finger bones (2 per finger + thumb).
- Flyers must regularly come into sword range; the player's weapon is the damage source (no arena tools).
- Owner's bars: smooth glossy materials (no mottled textures), no clipping, world palette, aggression matching attack speed.
- Weapons are sockets only (`Weapon_R` etc.) except a boss's unique weapon.
- Build and render headless: `blender -b --factory-startup --python assets/source/enemies/_framework/run.py -- ethereal_scape <enemy_id> --validate --render`

## Basic (5)
1. Aether Wisp (close range, hover: the tutorial enemy). BUILT: `aether_wisp.py`, small drifting glass-and-glow
   spirit, twin trailing tendrils, orbiting gold halo. Same archetype slot as Sky Citadel's Lantern Wisp
   (`hover_melee`) in this world's own palette. ~1.5k tris (body + glow).
2. Temple Acolyte (long range / support: chants a slow ranged bolt, breaks on approach). NOT BUILT YET.
   Humanoid, temple-ivory robes, a portal-glow chant sigil.
3. Meadow Stag (close range: charges in packs of two or three). NOT BUILT YET. Creature body, mint/gold hide,
   crystal antlers.
4. Crystal Warden (close range, heavy: tanky, shatters into shards on death). NOT BUILT YET. Humanoid, sky-crystal
   plating over a temple-ivory frame.
5. Skyborne Harrier (close range, flyer: dive, then lands and stalks). NOT BUILT YET. Creature body, same flyer
   contract as Sky Citadel's Aviary Harrier -- must come into sword range regularly.

## Minibosses (3)
- Waystone Sentinel (Waystone Ring) -- a waystone given a body, slow and heavy.
- Reliquary Keeper (Reliquary Court) -- guards the relics, punishing if approached carelessly.
- Gatewarden (either Temple Gate) -- the common obstacle before the Sanctum; two skins, one archetype.

All three NOT BUILT YET.

## Boss (1)
1. **The Ascendant** (the Sanctum). NOT BUILT YET. A temple guardian mid-transfiguration -- ivory-and-gold
   humanoid form giving way to raw sky crystal and portal glow at the extremities. Fast but fair moveset with
   real punish windows, per `boss-moveset-rules`. Weapon designed after the body once the body is final.
