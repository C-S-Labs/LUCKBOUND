# Ethereal Scape enemy roster (base chunk set only)

Drafted 2026-09-26, alongside the world's 30-piece chunk kit (see
`docs/biomes/ETHEREAL_SCAPE.md`). Same universal rules as every other biome's
roster (`docs/ENEMY_FRAMEWORK.md`):
- Basic/miniboss/legendary tri budgets: basic 10k-15k, miniboss <=35k, boss <=75k. Every mesh <10k tris.
- Rig: humanoid enemies get R15 core bones; minibosses and bosses get finger bones (2 per finger + thumb).
- Flyers must regularly come into sword range; the player's weapon is the damage source (no arena tools).
- Owner's bars: smooth glossy materials (no mottled textures), no clipping, world palette, aggression matching attack speed.
- Weapons are sockets only (`Weapon_R` etc.) except a boss's unique weapon.
- Build and render headless: `blender -b --factory-startup --python assets/source/enemies/_framework/run.py -- ethereal_scape <enemy_id> --validate --render`

## Design language (shared by every ES enemy; do NOT reskin Sky Citadel bodies)
- **Transfiguration**: temple-ivory + gold bodies that break apart into raw sky crystal at the extremities
  (hems, hands, antlers, wingtips). Builds towards the Ascendant, who is mid-transfiguration.
- **Gold masks**: smooth faceless gold faces split by a vertical portal-glow slit; no hoods, no void faces.
- **Halos**: an occasional accent (Wisp, Acolyte), never a default -- most ES enemies do NOT get one.
- **Ribbons**: mint/teal ribbon tendrils trailing off bodies and limbs, ending in portal glow.
- Hovering and drifting over stomping: the enemies feel weightless, like the islands.

## Basic (5)
1. Aether Wisp (close range, hover: the tutorial enemy). BUILT: `aether_wisp.py`, small drifting glass-and-glow
   spirit, twin trailing tendrils, orbiting gold halo. Same archetype slot as Sky Citadel's Lantern Wisp
   (`hover_melee`) in this world's own palette. ~1.5k tris (body + glow).
2. Temple Acolyte (long range / support: chants a slow ranged bolt, breaks on approach). Scripted (see Build status).
   Humanoid, temple-ivory robes, a portal-glow chant sigil.
3. Meadow Stag (close range: charges in packs of two or three). Scripted (see Build status). Creature body, mint/gold hide,
   crystal antlers.
4. Crystal Warden (close range, heavy: tanky, shatters into shards on death). Scripted (see Build status). Humanoid, sky-crystal
   plating over a temple-ivory frame.
5. Skyborne Harrier (close range, flyer: dive, then lands and stalks). Scripted (see Build status). Creature body, same flyer
   contract as Sky Citadel's Aviary Harrier -- must come into sword range regularly.

## Minibosses (3)
- Waystone Sentinel (Waystone Ring) -- a waystone given a body, slow and heavy.
- Reliquary Keeper (Reliquary Court) -- guards the relics, punishing if approached carelessly.
- Gatewarden (either Temple Gate) -- the common obstacle before the Sanctum; two skins, one archetype.

All three Scripted (see Build status).

## Boss (1)
1. **The Ascendant** (the Sanctum). BUILT (first pass, 2026-09-28): `the_ascendant.py` +
   `the_ascendant_staff.py`, moveset `ASCENDANT_MOVESET.md`, actions in `anims/the_ascendant/`. A temple guardian
   mid-transfiguration: ~3.5 m, slender ivory-and-gold body, gold slit mask, crystal horns and a shard cascade
   breaking out of the skull (no halo), crystal forearms/hands and shins/feet, a front-slit two-panel robe (the
   Acolyte's technique) shedding crystal from the hem, teal/mint ribbons. Phase 2 sheds the ivory cuirass
   (`Breakaway`) to show the portal core. ~18.5k tris (boss cap 75k).
   **Weapon: the Sanctum Staff**, weapon type **Staff** (`docs/WEAPONS.md`), so it can drop as the Ascendant's
   Legendary staff: crescent sky-crystal head (`Head`) around a portal-eye `Core`, on a gold/ivory `Shaft`.
   To do: the player-drop export (`wpn_es_staff_legendary_a`, Root/Shaft/Head/Core/Fx_Cast bones).
